# Reported and not established

## Reported in the notes in `evidence/`

- The notes report recorded runs on Canton DevNet and TestNet, using synthetic data and parties operated by the team.
- The notes report a login with a real Loop wallet on DevNet on 6 October 2026.

## Not established

- A full four-mode run with two real Loop wallets.
- Consent signed with a real wallet, or any ledger command sent by the wallet.
- Separation between operators. The team reports that the parties of the demonstration used one participant node operated by the team; this export does not independently establish separation between operators.
- That a declared figure is true. A coherent false statement made by every party would not be detected.
- Legal validity, custody, settlement or readiness for production use.
- Reproduction of any run from this repository.

This is not a pilot, and we know of no customer using it. A statement that was not independently checked is not claimed here.
